Take command of a virtual company network. An adaptive AI attacker chooses targets and attack types while you protect limited assets. Every choice changes the payoff and influences future rounds.

How to Play

  1. Read the threat-intelligence probabilities.
  2. Choose one defense before the attacker moves.
  3. The AI selects a target and attack based on its adaptive strategy.
  4. Your defense and the attack are evaluated using payoffs.
  5. Protect the network while managing limited defense credits.
  6. Use the final Game Theory report for your viva.

Educational game: it models cyber strategy without performing real attacks.
